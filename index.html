Rules

  • All players start at a random tile on the board with 3 hearts and 1 AP.
  • Anyone can act at any time. AP is granted at the scheduled refresh time, and the first refresh may come sooner than 24 hours from the start in standard mode.
  • Every refresh each living player gains 1 AP. Downed players keep their position and lose all AP permanently.
  • Actions include moving to an adjacent open square, attacking a target in range, upgrading range, gifting AP, gifting a heart, or doing nothing.
  • Range is 3 in each cardinal direction and 2 diagonally, creating a connected radial pattern around the player. Your current range is always highlighted.
  • If a player is reduced to 0 hearts they are downed. A heart gift can revive them if they're within range and have not been swallowed outside the zone.
  • Downed players form a jury and may vote daily for the player they want to give +1 AP. If a juror is revived before the next refresh, their vote is removed.
  • If the zone shrink setting is enabled, the board shows a light warning ring one refresh before the zone contracts. On the following refresh, the outer border becomes a red danger zone and starts dealing damage to anyone still inside it. Players outside the safe board lose 1 heart on each shrink cycle and are removed if they hit 0 hearts.
  • The lobby host controls the lobby settings before starting the match. Once a match starts, all players are moved to the live board page and can return to the hub without affecting their in-progress game.
